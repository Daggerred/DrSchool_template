  📍 QUICK GUIDE: Add Google Maps to Your Website
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Get Your Map Code
──────────────────────────
1. Open: https://www.google.com/maps
2. Search: "Your School Name and Address"
3. Click: "Share" button (left side)
4. Click: "Embed a map" tab
5. Click: "COPY HTML"

You'll get code like this:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ <iframe src="https://www.google.com/maps/embed?pb=...  │
│   width="600" height="450" style="border:0;"            │
│   allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy"></iframe>           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘


STEP 2: Replace in Your Website
────────────────────────────────
1. Open: index.html (in your school-ssds folder)
2. Find: Line ~845 (search for "REPLACE THIS IFRAME")
3. Replace: The entire <iframe> tag with your copied code


STEP 3: Update the Link
────────────────────────
Find this line (around line 855):
  <a href="https://maps.google.com/?q=YOUR_SCHOOL_ADDRESS"

Replace YOUR_SCHOOL_ADDRESS with your actual address:
  Example: Dr.+Rajendra+Prasad+School+Delhi
  (Use + instead of spaces)


DONE! 🎉
────────
Your map will now show on your website!
