Who We Are

A Tradition of Excellence

Founded in 1985, Dr. Rajendra Prasad Girija Devi Public School has grown from a community vision into one of the region's most respected institutions. We believe every child deserves an education that is rigorous in knowledge and generous in spirit.

Our CBSE-affiliated curriculum pairs disciplined academic study with a genuine commitment to sports, creative arts, and character development — because we know that the student who wins a debate or leads a house team is developing skills that no textbook can fully teach.

Education is not preparation for life; education is life itself. — School Philosophy

Academic Rigour

Structured learning aligned with CBSE best practices, with an emphasis on conceptual depth over memorisation.

Creative Arts

Music, fine arts, drama, and literary clubs nurtured alongside academics throughout the school year.

Sports Culture

Dedicated grounds, professional coaches, and a track record of district-to-national level competition.

Moral Values

Character education and community service built into daily school life — not treated as an afterthought.

Admissions 2025–26

We welcome students from all backgrounds. Admissions for the academic session 2025–26 are open across all classes. Seats are limited — we encourage early enquiry.

1

Fill the Enquiry Form

Submit the online enquiry on this page, or visit the school office between 8 AM – 2 PM, Monday to Saturday, to collect a printed prospectus.

2

School Visit & Interaction

Schedule a campus tour. Our admissions coordinator will take you through the school and answer all your questions in person.

3

Submit Required Documents

Bring previous year marksheets, transfer certificate (for Class II onwards), birth certificate, and two passport photographs.

4

Admission Confirmed

Upon document verification and fee payment, admission is confirmed. The school will share the academic calendar, uniform details, and booklist.

Request Information

Fill in your details and our admissions team will reach out within one working day.
